## Q1. Which faculty runs MA Buddhist Studies at Thammasat? Official Thai and English names and URL

### Takeaway
Thammasat appears to have offered a ศิลปศาสตรมหาบัณฑิต (ศศ.ม.) in พุทธศาสน์ศึกษา / พุทธศาสนศึกษา (Master of Arts Program in Buddhist Studies), probably in คณะศิลปศาสตร์ (Faculty of Liberal Arts). No official Thammasat page confirmed the host unit, and the program may now be closed.

### Cited Findings (all from search-result summaries; none of the pages were opened)
- A World Buddhist University directory page for Thammasat reportedly lists "Faculty of Liberal Arts. Department of Buddhist Studies - Master of Arts Program in Buddhist Studies." — [worldbuddhistuniversity.com](https://www.worldbuddhistuniversity.com/content/thammasat-university). This is a third-party directory and its date is unknown.
- The English Wikipedia article on Thammasat reportedly lists a master's in Buddhist Studies among the disciplines of its graduate school — [Wikipedia: Thammasat University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thammasat_University). This is not a primary source.
- A journal article cites a 2001 (B.E. 2544) thesis by พระมหานูพัฒน์ (Phramaha Noopat) Kongbang, "An analytical study of the critical reflection (Yonisomanasikara) in the Suttantapitaka", described as "Thesis Submitted in Master of Arts Program in Buddhist Studies: Thammasat University" — [ความจริงของชีวิตในทัศนะพุทธศาสนา, วารสารมนุษยศาสตร์ฯ (sujthai)](https://so05.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/sujthai/article/view/143400). This is consistent with the English program name "Master of Arts Program in Buddhist Studies".
- MCU curriculum documents (มคอ.2) list lecturers who hold "ศศ.ม. (พุทธศาสนศึกษา) มหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์ ๒๕๔๔" (พระมหาคาวี ญาณสาโร) and "ศศ.ม. (พุทธศาสนศึกษา) มหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์ ๒๕๕๐" (นายวุฒิชัย อ่าบำรุง). Another document gives "ศศ.ม. (พุทธศาสน์ศึกษา) มหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์ ๒๕๔๔" — [MCU Raikhing มคอ.2 พธ.ด. พระพุทธศาสนา 2560](https://raikhing.mcu.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%AD.-2-%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%98.%E0%B8%94.-%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B2-2560-%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%81.pdf); [MCU มคอ.2 พธ.ด. พระไตรปิฎกศึกษา](https://fb.mcu.ac.th/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/65_%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%AD2_%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%94_%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B9%84%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%8E%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%B6%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A9%E0%B8%B2_%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%93%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%B8%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%98.pdf). The search summary did not say which of the two documents holds which entry.
- The Thai Wikipedia article on คณะศิลปศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์ reportedly contains a truncated phrase, "ส่วนสาขาวิชาศาสนาได้ทำการปิดการเรียน". The search summary could not tell whether this refers to Buddhist Studies or when the closure happened — [Wikipedia: คณะศิลปศาสตร์ มธ.](https://th.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%93%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C_%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B4%E0%B8%97%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%98%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A8%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%AA%E0%B8%95%E0%B8%A3%E0%B9%8C)
- A Buddhist link directory (bhujati.org) lists "คณะศิลปศาสตร์ มหาวิทยาลัยธรรมศาสตร์" and a "ศูนย์พุทธศาสนศึกษา" for Thammasat. The relationship between the two is unclear — [bhujati.org](https://www.bhujati.org/)

### Inferences
- Theses were completed in this program in at least B.E. 2544, 2546 (see Q3) and 2550, so it ran for at least about 2001–2007.
- The Thai name is spelled both "พุทธศาสน์ศึกษา" and "พุทธศาสนศึกษา". Search catalogs under both spellings.
- The hint that the "religion" program closed, and the fact that no current Thammasat program page turned up in any search, suggest the program may no longer admit students. **Confirm this with Thammasat before planning a thesis around it.** If it is closed, a student may need a related, currently open Thammasat program, which this research did not identify.

### Gaps
- Not confirmed from any official tu.ac.th or arts.tu.ac.th page: the host faculty, the department, the official program URL, and whether the program is currently open. Tried: Thai and English web searches (7+ query variants) and direct fetches of arts.tu.ac.th, tu.ac.th, grad.tu.ac.th and reg.tu.ac.th, all of which were blocked.

## Q3. Past theses and independent studies from the program (titles, years, URLs) and dominant methods

### Takeaway
Only two theses could be identified, both through citations in other works. Neither was seen in TU Digital Collections or ThaiLIS. Both are textual and doctrinal studies of canonical texts. No thesis on temples, art or Bangkok sites from this program was found.

### Cited Findings
- พระมหานูพัฒน์ (Phramaha Noopat) Kongbang, B.E. 2544 (2001), "An analytical study of the critical reflection (Yonisomanasikara) in the Suttantapitaka" (การศึกษาวิเคราะห์โยนิโสมนสิการในพระสุตตันตปิฎก; the Thai title is a translation and was not seen verbatim), M.A. Program in Buddhist Studies, Thammasat University. The record comes from a citation, not a repository — [sujthai article citing it](https://so05.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/sujthai/article/view/143400)
- An MCU thesis PDF reportedly cites a Thammasat master's thesis in "พุทธศาสนศึกษา", B.E. 2546 (2003), from คณะศิลปศาสตร์. The search summary gave **no title or author**, and I could not open the PDF — [MCU e-thesis PDF](https://e-thesis.mcu.ac.th/storage/RI1WiXkaLDC6tEzwz3m98YjEF6yGrvhWu67nHHST.pdf)
- A TCI article surveys graduate theses of the **Thai language** program (สาขาวิชาภาษาไทย) of คณะศิลปศาสตร์ มธ. for academic years 2545–2553. It is useful only as a model of the faculty's thesis culture. It is not about Buddhist Studies — [วรรณวิทัศน์: วิทยานิพนธ์ สาขาวิชาภาษาไทย คณะศิลปศาสตร์ มธ. 2545-2553](https://so06.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/VANNAVIDAS/article/view/68841)

### Inferences
- From this very small sample (one titled thesis), the program's work looks text-based: analysis of Pali-canon doctrine. That would favour a Wat Suthat topic with a textual core, such as inscriptions, jataka murals read against texts, or Rattanakosin-era Buddhist texts tied to the temple, over purely architectural or art-historical surveys. **This is a weak inference from n=1.**

### Gaps
- TU Digital Collections (digital.library.tu.ac.th) and ThaiLIS TDC (tdc.thailis.or.th) were unreachable, so no repository listing, full title list or URLs could be produced. Suggested searches once access is available: degree "ศิลปศาสตรมหาบัณฑิต" with discipline "พุทธศาสน์ศึกษา" or "พุทธศาสนศึกษา"; keywords วัด, จิตรกรรม, ศิลาจารึก, กรุงรัตนโกสินทร์, วัดสุทัศน์.
- No temple, art, text or Bangkok-site thesis from this program was found. This reflects the lack of access, not evidence that none exist.
