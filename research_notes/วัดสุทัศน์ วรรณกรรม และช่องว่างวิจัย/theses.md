## 1. Bibliography: which theses exist on Wat Suthat and its components?

### Takeaway
Only **two** theses with Wat Suthat as their main subject could be confirmed from repository index entries. Both study the **murals of the พระวิหารหลวง**:
- a Chulalongkorn thesis on the ไตรภูมิ/โลกสัณฐาน paintings on the vihara pillars;
- a Silpakorn (Faculty of Archaeology) study of the อดีตพระพุทธเจ้า 27 พระองค์ murals.

A third, a 1998 Silpakorn thesis by จิรศักดิ์ แต่งเจนกิจ on the temple's architecture, is cited in two scholarly sources, but I could not see its own repository record.

No thesis was found on any of these topics:
- the Buddha images: พระศรีศากยมุนี, พระพุทธตรีโลกเชษฐ์, พระพุทธเสฏฐมุนี, the gallery images
- สัตตมหาสถาน
- ตุ๊กตาศิลาจีน
- the Rama II doors
- the Giant Swing and Brahmin connection
- the Pali school
- สมเด็จพระสังฆราช (แพ)
- พระกริ่ง
- rituals
- tourism, management, or Chinese-language services

- University: Chulalongkorn University. The record is in the CU ETD collection (chulaetd).
- English title: "Cosmology system of the mural painting in Wat Suthat Thepwararamratchaworamahawihan", as quoted in the search summary.
- Year: พ.ศ. 2562 / ค.ศ. 2019. This comes from the search-engine summary only, not from the record.
- Faculty/program, degree and advisor: ไม่พบข้อมูล. The program is probably art history, but that is unverified.
- Record: [CU ETD record](https://digital.car.chula.ac.th/chulaetd/9192/)
- Summary from the abstract, as quoted in search snippets:
  - The thesis studies the Tribhumi-Lokasanthana cosmology mural in the พระวิหารหลวง, painted around the early Rattanakosin period (19th century).
  - The mural is unusual because it is painted on **8 square pillars (4 pairs, 32 faces)** instead of the walls. This turns a 2-D surface into a 3-D arrangement.
  - Its content is said to be correlated with **Tipitaka, Atthakatha and Lokasatra (โลกศาสตร์)**.
  - It matches the Hotrai (หอไตร) of Wat Rakhang-Kositaram and illustrated Tribhum manuscripts of the Ayutthaya, Thonburi and Rattanakosin periods in order, composition, content and style.
- Article derived from the thesis: "COSMOLOGY SYSTEM OF THE MURAL PAINTING IN WAT SUTHAT THEPWARARAM RATCHAWORAMAHAWIHAN BANGKOK", co-authored with Jeerawan Sangpetch, in the FAA journal. [Journal article](https://so02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/faa/article/view/241295)

**A2. การวิเคราะห์จิตรกรรมฝาผนังเรื่องอดีตพระพุทธเจ้าในวิหารหลวงวัดสุทัศนเทพวราราม**

- University/faculty: Silpakorn University, Faculty of Archaeology.
- English title, as quoted in the search summary: "Analysis of mural paint of the previous Buddha's life at Wat Suthat Thepphawararam".
- Year: 2012 (≈ พ.ศ. 2555). This comes from the search summary only.
- Degree: **conflicting**.
  - One search summary says the collection is "Theses (Master's degree) – Art History".
  - Another calls it "สารนิพนธ์".
  - Check on the record.
- Advisor: ไม่พบข้อมูล.
- Records: [SURE record](https://sure.su.ac.th/xmlui/handle/123456789/1585?attempt=3&attempt=2); [author browse page](https://sure.su.ac.th/xmlui/handle/123456789/905/browse?value=%E0%B8%A5%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%94%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%81%E0%B8%A3+%E0%B9%80%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B5%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%9B%E0%B8%A3%E0%B8%B0%E0%B8%9E%E0%B8%B1%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%98%E0%B9%8C&type=author)
- Summary from the abstract, as quoted in snippets:
  - The study covers the murals of all **27 past Buddhas** in the วิหารหลวง.
  - The painters mainly chose two scenes for each Buddha: birth/social status and the Great Renunciation (ออกมหาภิเนษกรมณ์).
  - The murals are "one of the most correct and complete mural paintings, based on Tripitaka".
  - Their central concept comes from the "centre of the universe" ideology.
  - The style shows the influence of Thai and Sri Lankan Buddhist exchange.

### Inferences
- The thesis literature I could confirm is small and concentrated on **one building (the วิหารหลวง) and one medium (murals)**, studied through art history and archaeology.

## 2. Which disciplines dominate, and what is missing from a Buddhist-textual angle?

### Takeaway
**Art history and archaeology (Silpakorn, Chulalongkorn) dominate.** Every confirmed thesis is a mural study.

No thesis was found on these:
- tourism or management
- Buddhist Studies degrees (พธ.ม. at MCU, ศน.ม. at MBU)
- architecture as a separate field, apart from the unverified 1998 Taengjanekij thesis

From a Buddhist-textual angle, the murals have been **related to** texts (Tipiṭaka, Aṭṭhakathā, Lokasatra, Tribhum). But no thesis was found that studies the texts themselves on site: for example, the **Buddhavaṃsa-commentary inscriptions** under the vihara murals, or the Pali and Thai sources behind the ไตรภูมิ pillars.

### Cited Findings
- The pillar cosmology mural is "correlated to Buddhist scripture such as Tipitaka, Attakatha, Lokasatra". The thesis compares it with Tribhum manuscripts and the Wat Rakhang Hotrai, which is an art-historical comparison. [CU ETD record](https://digital.car.chula.ac.th/chulaetd/9192/); [FAA journal article](https://so02.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/faa/article/view/241295)
- The past-Buddha murals are judged "based on Tripitaka" and on cosmic-centre ideology. The analysis selects scenes (birth and the Great Renunciation). [SURE record](https://sure.su.ac.th/xmlui/handle/123456789/1585?attempt=3&attempt=2)
- Schmidt (2010) notes the **Buddhavaṃsa commentary inscriptions** below the vihara murals and reads them as asserting "presence" rather than teaching. This is the only work found that treats the inscriptions as an object of study. [Wiley record](https://compass.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/j.1749-8171.2010.00242.x)
- Amaranonta argues that Thai past-Buddha and Three-Worlds murals take their stories from Thai Buddhist literature. That is a general argument, not specific to Wat Suthat. [Journal article](https://so01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/damrong/article/view/20917)
- A Silpakorn thesis compares the Buddhaisawan chapel murals with Buddhist scriptures, showing that a "mural vs. scripture" design exists for another royal site. [Silpakorn DSpace record](http://ithesis-ir.su.ac.th/dspace/handle/123456789/1852)
- No thesis was found in searches on the following topics; in each case the results were only news, travel, museum or amulet-trade pages:
  - **สมเด็จพระสังฆราช (แพ ติสฺสเทว)**: results were news about the new ตำหนัก museum (opened 6 ธ.ค. 2567) and biography pages. Examples: [Thai Post](https://www.thaipost.net/news-update/711232/), [Thammapedia](https://thammapedia.com/sankha/sangharaja_12.php), [SAC museum DB](https://db.sac.or.th/museum/museum-detail/1635). Press reports mention **historic palm-leaf manuscripts (คัมภีร์ใบลาน)** in the museum. [Bangkok Biz News](https://www.bangkokbiznews.com/blogs/lifestyle/art-living/1173441)
  - **พระกริ่ง**: only amulet-column and trade pages. [Matichon Weekly](https://www.matichon.co.th/weekly/column/article_311558)
  - **ตุ๊กตาศิลาจีน**: only feature articles. [Dailynews](https://www.dailynews.co.th/articles/1287931/)
  - **สัตตมหาสถาน, the gallery Buddha images, พระพุทธตรีโลกเชษฐ์, the carved doors, เสาชิงช้า/โบสถ์พราหมณ์**: only general or encyclopedic pages. [Matichon Academy, the doors](https://www.matichonacademy.com/tour-story/%E0%B8%8A%E0%B8%A1-%E0%B8%84%E0%B8%A7%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%A1%E0%B8%A2%E0%B8%B7%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%AB%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%B6%E0%B9%88%E0%B8%87%E0%B8%9A%E0%B8%B2%E0%B8%99%E0%B8%9B); [SU Rattanakosin resource on the Brahmin-shrine community](http://www.resource.lib.su.ac.th/rattanakosin/index.php/2014-10-27-08-52-32/2015-10-21-08-18-25)

### Inferences
These are open thesis topics suited to Buddhist Studies, with no prior thesis found. Absence is relative to the searchable web index only.

1. **A critical edition and translation of the Buddhavaṃsa-aṭṭhakathā (Madhuratthavilāsinī) inscriptions in the พระวิหารหลวง**, and their correspondence with each of the 27 past-Buddha panels. This extends Schmidt (2010) and Niemprapan (A2) from the text side. It is probably the strongest gap, and it fits a พุทธศาสนศึกษา degree well.
2. **The Pali and Thai textual sources of the ไตรภูมิ pillars.** Which recension is it: Traibhūmikathā, Traiphum Lok Winitchai (ไตรภูมิโลกวินิจฉยกถา, of the Rama I era), or Lokapaññatti? A1 compares it with manuscripts by composition and style, but apparently not line by line against the texts. That needs checking in the full text.
3. **The พระอุโบสถ murals** (and อดีตพุทธ 28 / พระอสีติมหาสาวก) **read against the Pali canon and Thai literature**. So far they have only been studied through the art-theory "time and space" lens.
4. **The Buddhist doctrine and Pali scholarship of the abbots and Supreme Patriarchs** (e.g. สมเด็จพระสังฆราช แพ, ป.ธ.5), including the newly exhibited palm-leaf manuscripts in the ตำหนัก museum. This is a new primary-source corpus, opened in 2024.
5. **พระกริ่ง as ritual practice.** Its consecration liturgy, the texts chanted, and the doctrine of protective power (ปริตร) could be studied through Buddhist textual and anthropological lenses rather than collector discourse.
6. **The Buddhist-Brahmanical relationship around เสาชิงช้า, โบสถ์พราหมณ์ and พิธีน้ำอภิเษก**: how Thai Buddhist texts and rites frame it.
7. **สัตตมหาสถาน** (the seven places of the first seven weeks after enlightenment) **compared with the Jātaka-nidāna and Paṭhamasambodhi narratives**.

### Gaps
- I could not read the full texts of A1 and A2 to confirm how deeply they engage the Pali sources. Gap 2 above in particular should be checked against A1's bibliography.
- I could not confirm whether the 27 Buddhavaṃsa inscriptions have already been transcribed in a Fine Arts Department or temple publication rather than a thesis.

## 3. Thammasat theses on Wat Suthat, or on comparable Bangkok royal temples

### Takeaway
**No Thammasat thesis on Wat Suthat was found.** Thammasat's repository could not be accessed. Site-restricted searches of digital.library.tu.ac.th surfaced only cremation/memorial volumes, not theses. No Thammasat thesis on comparable royal temples surfaced either.

### Cited Findings
- A domain-restricted search of digital.library.tu.ac.th for "วัดสุทัศน์" returned memorial books, not theses. Examples: [TU record dc:311883](https://digital.library.tu.ac.th/tu_dc/frontend/Info/item/dc:311883); [TU record dc:6698](http://digital.library.tu.ac.th/tu_dc/frontend/Info/item/dc:6698)
- A broader search for Thammasat theses on Bangkok royal temples returned none. The nearest academic results were from elsewhere:
  - a TU Delft thesis on temple and community in Rattanakosin (Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Arun, Wat Pho) [TU Delft repository](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_10df9f5e-1daa-41ae-a9de-cdd1b5860c00)
  - a Chulalongkorn journal article on the "forgotten heritage" of Rattanakosin [CU journal PDF](https://www.cujucr.com/downloads/Individual%20Articles/9/vol9%20Piyamas%20Lernapakun.pdf)

### Inferences
- Thammasat's strengths are history (คณะศิลปศาสตร์), Thai studies and anthropology. A Thammasat thesis on Wat Suthat would more likely be historical or social, about the temple, the court and the community, than about art history. The search could not confirm whether one exists.
- A Buddhist Studies student at Thammasat would face little overlap with existing work if they took a text-centred topic, such as items 1–4 in Section 2.

### Gaps
- digital.library.tu.ac.th and TU's thesis catalogue need a direct search, in a browser or with the host allowed, using: "วัดสุทัศน", "สุทัศนเทพวราราม", "เสาชิงช้า", "วัดหลวง", "วัดประจำรัชกาล", "พระอารามหลวง กรุงเทพ".
- These databases also need direct searching: MCU e-thesis (e-thesis.mcu.ac.th), MBU (gs.mbuisc.ac.th/th/ethesis/), ThaiLIS TDC, SURE (all faculties, not only Archaeology), CU ETD, and ProQuest.
